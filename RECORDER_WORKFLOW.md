# Recording workflow

Use SETUP beside RECORD in Chart Studio, or TAKE SETUP on the recorder. The same section is available through Chart Tools → Recording.

1. Select a time range, or set editor A / B. Enable punch-in to use those bounds. Without punch-in, recording runs from the playhead to the song end.
2. Choose Layer or Replace. Replace affects only unlocked types belonging to the selected recording pass, inside the punch boundaries. Earlier sustained notes crossing the boundary remain intact. Empty takes keep existing notes.
3. Set audible pre-roll to 0–4 bars of four beats. The normal countdown comes first; the music then plays the available lead-in. Input is ignored until the punch start.
4. Choose Free timing, Soft Snap (halfway to the grid), or Strict Snap. The recording subdivision uses the chart's BPM and first-beat offset. Sustained-note paths and checkpoint offsets follow duration changes.
5. Record all types, or make separate Tap/Hold/Slide/Flick passes. Lock completed types to protect them from replacement and corrections.
6. Enable the rhythm guide for an accented downbeat, beat/eighth/sixteenth subdivisions, and separate guide volume.
7. CORRECT pauses the transport and lets you move or convert notes without leaving the recorder. RECORD resumes from the correction playhead. Locked types cannot be edited.
8. With Loop Takes enabled, reaching the end banks the attempt and restarts at the same section with pre-roll. STOP opens review; a session banks up to 32 attempts.
9. Review highlights the provisional notes in gold. Replay temporarily opens the entire timeline, then returns to review. Choose a take, compare it against the previous attempt or original section, or splice the first part of one attempt with the second part of another at a chosen song time.
10. Keep accepts the provisional take into the draft. Retry restores the earlier chart while retaining the session's take bank. Undo Take restores the earlier chart in one action. Quality flags identify overlaps, likely double taps, short sustains and rapid slide motion. SAVE publishes the draft.

Recorder pause actions are Resume Rec, Retry Take, and Review Take. Resume continues recording; Retry restarts the current section; Review opens the editor's take review instead of the lobby.

Take banks are temporary session data. New sessions replace the bank. Finished provisional notes are autosaved as draft notes, and undoing/retrying updates that draft too. Recorder setup changes apply to the next new session; loop attempts keep the session's original configuration.

Global recorder preferences are also available under Settings → Editor Prefs → Recorder Preferences. Session range, pass, locks, replacement and loop-take choices are kept separate from global preferences and chart metadata.

# Note appearance

Settings → Visual Effects includes independent Note Size (60–125%) and Hold Trail Width (30–150%) sliders, a live preview, and Reset Note Sizes. Changes apply to gameplay and recording visuals and persist globally. Slide widths, musical timing, lane centers and hit detection are unaffected.
